# Proposal templates

## A. Full proposal (email or PDF, 700 to 1,100 words)

```
# [Outcome] for [Company]
Prepared for [Name], [Role] — [DD/MM/YYYY] — valid until [DD/MM/YYYY]

## The outcome
In [timeframe], [Company] will have [specific measurable result].

## What I saw
- [Observed fact 1, with where you saw it]
- [Observed fact 2]
- [Observed fact 3]

## What this is costing now
[Problem] is costing roughly [number] per month, based on [stated basis].
Assumption: [any figure you estimated, labelled clearly].

## The plan
### Phase 1 — [Name] (days 1 to X)
Work: [what happens]
Handover: [artefact]

### Phase 2 — [Name] (days X to Y)
Work:
Handover:

### Phase 3 — [Name] (days Y to Z)
Work:
Handover:

## Included
- [Deliverable]
- [Deliverable]
- [Deliverable]

## Not included
- [Exclusion]
- [Exclusion]
Anything outside this list is quoted separately before any work starts.

## Timeline
Signature and deposit by [date]. Start [date]. Phase 1 delivered [date]. Complete by [date].

## Investment
| Option | What you get | Price | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | [scope] | [price] | [weeks] |
| Complete (recommended) | [scope] | [price] | [weeks] |
| Complete plus support | [scope] + [months] of support | [price] | [weeks] |

Terms: [x]% on signature, [y]% on Phase 2 handover, balance on completion. Price held until [date].

## Your risk
[One concrete reversal you can honour.]

## Proof
[Relevant example, what changed, verifiable link. If none, offer the paid pilot.]

## Why me for this
[Four lines maximum, all about their problem.]

## Next step
[One action]. I have held [date and time] for a 20 minute call: [link].
```

## B. Marketplace bid (150 to 250 words)

```
[Their exact problem in one line, quoted back sharper than they wrote it.]

I looked at [specific thing you checked] and noticed [observed detail]. That usually means [diagnosis].

Here is how I would handle it:
1. [Step, with output]
2. [Step, with output]
3. [Step, with output]

Timeline: [x] days. Price: [number] fixed, [deposit terms].
Not included: [one exclusion], quoted separately.

Closest match from my work: [one line plus link].

One question so I can be precise: [a question only someone who read the brief would ask].

[Name]
```

Never open a bid with "Dear Sir/Madam", "I am the perfect fit", or a skills list.

## C. Direct message (under 120 words)

```
[Name] — noticed [specific observed detail] on [where].

[One line on what it is probably costing.]

I fix exactly this for [buyer type]. Fixed [price], [x] days, [risk reversal].

Worth 15 minutes on [day]? If not, I will send a two minute teardown video anyway, no strings.
```

## D. Follow-up sequence

Day 3:
```
[Name], one thing I forgot to include: [new specific insight about their situation].
Still happy to run this for [price] with the [date] start. Yes or no is fine.
```

Day 7:
```
I had a look at [competitor or adjacent example] doing [thing]. Attaching the 3 differences I would apply to you.
The [date] slot is open until [date].
```

Day 14:
```
Closing the file on this one so it stops sitting in your inbox. If the timing changes, the scope and price stand until [date]. Either way, here is the checklist I would have used: [link].
```

## E. Assumptions block, always visible

```
## Assumptions
- [Assumption], which changes the price by [amount] if wrong
- Access needed: [list]
- Client responsibilities: [list], with [x] working day turnaround on feedback
```
