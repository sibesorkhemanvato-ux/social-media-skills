## Direct B2B signals

- LinkedIn job search for the same role, filtered to the last 7 days. Three posts of the same role in six months means budget exists.
- Company careers pages in the target sector.
- Reddit and niche forums: search `[topic] "looking for" OR "anyone know a"` in the last month.
- X and Telegram channels where the buyer complains about the problem.
- Google with a date filter: `"[topic]" ("we need" OR "hiring" OR "quote") past month`.

## Productised and digital

- Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy category bestsellers, with price and review counts.
- Notion, Figma and template marketplaces.
- Course platforms for topic demand and price ceilings.
- Existing paid communities in the niche and their monthly fee.

## Evidence capture format

Log every opened listing in `outputs/offer-scout/market-evidence.md`:

```
| Source | Title | URL | Posted (DD/MM/YYYY) | Budget | Proposals | Client history | Skills | Pain in client's words | Tags |
```

Tags are one or more of: repeat-demand, underserved, unbundleable.
